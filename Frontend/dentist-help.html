Appointment Management

Follow these steps to manage your appointments

Dashboard View the current and next scheduled appointment from the dashboard.
My Appointments Open My Appointments to view the scheduled patient appointment details.
Check Patient Review the patient's name, contact number, dentist, treatment, date and time.
Complete Appointment After checking the patient, click the Done button to mark the appointment as completed.
Next Appointment The next scheduled appointment can be viewed from the Dentist Dashboard.

Important Instructions

Please follow the system procedures carefully

Check Appointment Details Always verify the appointment information before checking the patient.
Use Done Carefully Click Done only after the patient's appointment has been completed.
Logout Logout from the system after finishing your work.
